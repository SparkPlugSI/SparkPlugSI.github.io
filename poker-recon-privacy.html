Poker Recon Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Poker Recon is a private log for your own poker results and for notes you choose to write about people you play with. This policy matches the app with bundle id com.sparkplugsi.pokerrecon.

What the app collects

The app collects nothing. It has no account, no server, and no network connection. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other code that sends data off the iPhone.

What stays on this iPhone

Dates, stakes, buy-ins, cash-outs, prizes, hours, place names, opponent names, and notes are stored only in the app’s sandbox, in the on-device database. A place name is text you type. The app does not use Location Services, contacts, photos, the camera, or the microphone.

Notes about other people are your private notes. They are not uploaded, published, or shared with those people or with anyone else.

Tracking and sharing

The app does not track you. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not ask for tracking permission. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to any third party, including any analytics, advertising, or AI service. There is no sale of data.

How long it stays, and how to delete it

The app does not ask you to consent to collection, because it does not collect data. There is no off-device copy to revoke.

Notes stay on the iPhone until you delete them in the app, or until you delete the app. Deleting a session or a note removes that entry from the on-device database. Deleting the app deletes its sandbox, including the database.

Children

Poker Recon is not directed at children. It still collects no personal information.
