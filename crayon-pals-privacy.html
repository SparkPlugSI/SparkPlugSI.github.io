Crayon Pals Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Crayon Pals is a coloring, doodle, and tracing app for young children.

What we collect

Crayon Pals does not collect personal information. It does not collect drawings, names, photos, location, device identifiers, contacts, or any other data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Where drawings live

Drawings saved in the app stay on this iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They are not uploaded, shared, or added to the Photos library. The app does not ask for permission to use the camera, microphone, photos, or location. Deleting a drawing in the Gallery removes that drawing. Deleting the app removes the drawings that are left.

Sound

A short celebration sound can play on this iPhone when a tracing page is finished. A grown-up can turn sound off in Settings. The app does not listen to the microphone.

Children

The app is made for children ages 2 to 6. It does not ask for a name, email address, or any other contact information, and it does not contact any other service. This policy is written to be friendly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act: the app does not collect personal information from children, and it does not share information with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text ships inside the app. The app does not open a website.
